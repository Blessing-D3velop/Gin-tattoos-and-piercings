AFTERCARE

TAKE CARE OF YOUR INK.

A tattoo is an open wound for the first few days. Proper aftercare keeps lines crisp, colour solid and your skin healthy while it heals.

These are general guidelines. Always follow the specific instructions your artist gives you for your piece and your skin — and contact us straight away if anything looks or feels wrong.

01 ✓
CLEAN

KEEP IT CLEAN.

Leave the wrap on as instructed, then wash gently with clean hands and unscented soap.

02 +
MOISTURISE

MOISTURISE LIGHTLY.

Apply a thin layer of aftercare balm two to three times a day. Never smother the tattoo.

03 ☀
PROTECT

NO SUN, NO SOAKING.

Avoid pools, baths and direct sun while healing. Use sunscreen once your tattoo is fully healed.

04 !
HEALING

DON'T PICK.

Flaking and itching are normal. Picking or scratching can pull ink out and cause scarring.
